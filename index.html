Why Cybersecurity Matters

People use websites every day to communicate, shop, study, and manage personal information. Understanding cybersecurity helps users recognize suspicious activity and make safer decisions online. Learning how websites protect data is also important for developing safer and more trustworthy digital experiences.

Start by exploring common cybersecurity threats, then learn how to protect websites and personal information.
