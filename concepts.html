Key Cybersecurity Concepts

The following terms provide a foundation for understanding how digital information and systems can be protected.

1. Cybersecurity

Cybersecurity is the practice of protecting systems, networks, applications, and data from digital attacks, unauthorized access, damage, or disruption.

2. Phishing

Phishing is a deceptive technique in which attackers pretend to be trustworthy sources to trick people into revealing sensitive information or clicking harmful links.

3. Malware

Malware is software created to perform harmful or unauthorized actions, such as stealing information, disrupting systems, or gaining access to a device.

4. Encryption

Encryption transforms readable information into a protected form that can be understood only by someone with the appropriate decryption method or key.

5. Firewall

A firewall monitors and filters network traffic according to security rules. It can help block unwanted connections while allowing permitted communications.

6. Authentication

Authentication is the process of verifying someone's identity before allowing access to an account, application, or system. Passwords and multifactor authentication are examples of authentication methods.

7. HTTPS

HTTPS is the secure version of HTTP. It uses TLS to protect data exchanged between a web browser and a website against certain forms of interception or tampering.

How These Concepts Work Together

Authentication helps control who can sign in, encryption protects information, and firewalls filter network traffic. Recognizing phishing and malware can help users avoid threats, while HTTPS helps protect information exchanged with websites.
